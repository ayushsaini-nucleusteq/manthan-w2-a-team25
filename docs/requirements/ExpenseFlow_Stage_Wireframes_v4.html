ExpenseFlow stage wireframes by role

Login is by credentials only, and the screen does not show who is signing in. The account's role decides which screens open. A person who holds more than one role (for example, a line manager who also files claims) sees a role switch in the top bar. The switch changes the screens shown, not the account. Amounts are in INR.

Role switch (for accounts with more than one role)

The switch appears only when the account holds more than one role. For a person with one role, the top bar shows just their name and role. The switch shows the role names the account holds, and the current one is highlighted.
